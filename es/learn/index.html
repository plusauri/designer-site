What do the colors mean?

Almost every lesson here reads with no background at all, and none of them are homework — you never have to understand a lesson to use the tool. A few cards carry an Intermediate or Advanced mark. Those are not warnings. They are the lessons where the product does something most planning tools cannot do at all, and the mark is there so you can go looking for them on purpose.
