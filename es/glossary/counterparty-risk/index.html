counterparty risk

Finanzas

The risk that the institution that owes you money cannot pay in full. A private pension is a promise from an employer's plan. An annuity is a promise from an insurance company. A bank deposit is a promise from a bank. Most of these promises are kept, but each depends on the institution behind it. Each kind has a backstop with limits. The Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation (PBGC) takes over failed private pension plans and pays benefits up to a yearly maximum, which can be less than the pension promised. State guaranty associations protect annuity owners when an insurer fails, up to limits set by each state. The FDIC insures bank deposits up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank, for each ownership category. Spreading large annuity purchases across insurers, and large deposits across banks, keeps more of the money under those limits. Tutilis does not model an institution failing: a pension or annuity in a plan pays as written.
