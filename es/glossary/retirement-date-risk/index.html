retirement date risk

Finanzas

The chance that a bad market in the last years of work pushes the retirement date back. Near the end of a career the portfolio is so large that one poor year can undo several years of saving, so two savers with the same average return can reach their goal years apart depending on when the poor years fall. Michael Kitces named it in a 2016 article, *Retirement Date Risk and Sequence of Returns*. It is the working-years side of sequence of returns, and holding less stock as the date approaches is the usual answer when the plan needs only a modest return.

Dónde está en la aplicación

Dónde se ingresa

  • Lowering stock toward the date is the usual answer.

Dónde aparece en el pronóstico

  • End the horizon at the retirement year to read the spread there.
