Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT)

3.8% surtax on the lesser of net investment income or MAGI over a filing-status threshold.

Nominal

Source: Form 8960 — Net Investment Income Tax — Internal Revenue Service

https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-8960

Where we use this · Used in your forecast

  • The 3.8% rate is applied to the lesser of net investment income or MAGI above the filing-status threshold, and lands as its own line on the federal tax statement — never folded into ordinary or capital-gains tax.
  • The Cliff radar tracks MAGI against the threshold so the year a plan crosses it is visible before it happens.

Not modeled

Full Form 8960 deductions and expenses; active-trader and material-participation carve-outs; estates and trusts; interaction with AMT credits.
