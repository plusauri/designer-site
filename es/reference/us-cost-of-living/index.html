Cost of living & wage indices

All-items and ex-housing price levels, local wage level, and median household income by locality — all normalized to the national average = 1.00.

Published 2024Held

Source: Regional Price Parities by State and Metro Area — U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis

https://www.bea.gov/data/prices-inflation/regional-price-parities-state-and-metro-area

Where we use this · Used in comparisons

  • Drives the US States relocation comparison: moving A→B scales living expenses by the ratio of the two localities' indices and labor income by the wage-index ratio.
  • The baseline forecast does not apply a cost-of-living multiplier — this only enters when you run a relocation what-if.
  • The ex-housing index is what a relocation uses, because the plan already carries the home as an asset and its property tax separately.

Not modeled

The **ex-housing** index is the one a relocation should use: the plan already carries the home as an asset and its property tax as a separate effective rate, so an all-items multiplier would count housing twice. Both are shown so that choice is visible rather than buried.
