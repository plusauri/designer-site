Retirement contribution limits

The IRS annual limits for 401(k)-family deferrals, IRAs and SIMPLE plans, with the age-50 catch-up and the SECURE 2.0 age 60–63 higher catch-up.

Published 2023–2026Held

Source: Notice 2025-67 — 2026 limitations for retirement plans and IRAs — Internal Revenue Service

https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500

Where we use thisShown, not applied

  • The Year card shows each working owner's catch-up amount and marks the year a new tier begins.
  • A contribution above the limit for its account type and the owner's age raises an authoring warning. The forecast still simulates the amount the plan states.

Not modeled

The forecast does not cap contributions at these limits. Also not modeled: the 2026 Roth catch-up mandate for high earners, §415(c) annual additions, plan-document limits below the statutory ones, and IRA deductibility or Roth eligibility phase-outs.
