State income tax

Per-state rate ladders, standard deduction, retirement-income treatment, and local income tax where it exists.

Published 2025Indexed

Where we use thisUsed in your forecast

  • Assessed each forecast year for the household's state of residence, producing the annual state tax statement where the state levies one.
  • The state's retirement-income treatment decides whether Social Security and pension income are taxed at all.
  • Local income tax is assessed where the state has one; brackets grow only on the axes the state actually indexes.

Not modeled

All 50 states are bundled. Federal-tax deductibility is modeled where it exists — Alabama in full, Missouri and Oregon on the income-tested ladders their forms publish. Municipal bond interest is added back in the states that tax it; the forecast does not distinguish in-state issuers, so in-state interest that most states leave exempt is treated as taxable. US Treasury interest is subtracted in every taxing state (federal law), from the holding's US-government-obligation fraction (Treasury and TIPS growth categories default to 100%). Still out of scope: tax credits other than the personal exemption, deduction and exemption phaseouts at high income, per-spouse computations in the states that tax a couple's incomes separately, and city-level rules beyond the single representative local rate.
