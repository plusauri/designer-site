Early withdrawal penalty (§72(t))

The 10% additional tax on pre-59½ distributions from traditional retirement accounts.

Not year-keyed

Source: IRC §72(t) — 10-percent additional tax on early distributions from qualified retirement plans — U.S. Code

https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/72

Where we use this · Used in your forecast

  • Applied when the plan sells from a traditional retirement account before the owner reaches 59½.
  • The 10% lands as its own line on the federal tax statement and as its own lane on the Cliff radar.

Not modeled

Every statutory exemption — rule of 55, SEPP, disability, medical expenses, higher education, first home, IRS levy, birth or adoption, terminal illness, federally declared disaster — plus the SIMPLE IRA 25% first-two-years rate and state add-ons. Plans that would qualify still show the base 10%.
