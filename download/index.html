Download and updates

Already bought Tutilis?

Your installers are kept with your order at Lemon Squeezy, which runs our checkout. To get the latest version:

  1. Open My Orders and sign in with the email you used at checkout. Lemon Squeezy emails you a sign-in link, so there is no password to remember.
  2. Open your Tutilis order and download the installer for your computer, Mac or Windows.
  3. Install it over the version you have. Your plans and your license are stored on your computer apart from the app, so they carry over.

Every purchase includes 12 months of updates. Tutilis checks for a new version when it starts and shows a small notice beside its name in the toolbar when one is out.

On the free trial?

Your installers are kept with your $0 trial order at Lemon Squeezy, the same way as a purchase: open My Orders and sign in with the email you gave.

When you buy, Lemon Squeezy emails you a license key. Click the padlock at the top of the app, paste the key, and choose Activate. You keep the app you have and the plans you made during the trial, with nothing to reinstall.

Not a customer yet?

You can use Tutilis free for 14 days. See Pricing for what it includes and how the trial works, or try the ideas first in the Learning Center.

Trouble getting the download?

Write to dan@plusauri.com with the email you bought with, and we will sort it out.
