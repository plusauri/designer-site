behavior risk

Finanzen

The risk that the investor, not the market, does the damage. The common forms are selling after a fall, buying more of whatever rose last year, and abandoning a plan in the year it is hardest to keep. A fall that is sat through is temporary. A fall that is sold is permanent, because the recovery that follows happens without the money that left. The gap between what a fund earns and what its investors earn, because they bought and sold at the wrong times, is sometimes called the behavior gap. It is hard to measure exactly, but studies of fund flows have found it to be large in volatile years. A forecast does not panic: it follows its rules every year. That is why the usual protection is to write the rules down before they are needed: a target mix, when to rebalance, and what to do in a crash. In Tutilis the target mix lives in the plan's glide path.
