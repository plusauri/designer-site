Federal poverty guidelines (%FPL)

HHS annual guideline ladders for the 48 contiguous states, Alaska, and Hawaii — the denominator behind %FPL.

Published 2025–2026Held

Source: HHS Poverty Guidelines — U.S. Department of Health and Human Services / ASPE

https://aspe.hhs.gov/topics/poverty-economic-mobility/poverty-guidelines

Where we use this · Shown, not applied

  • A %FPL ratio is computed for each forecast year from the plan's own household size and shown beside MAGI on the Cliff radar.
  • No subsidy, benefit, or eligibility amount is derived from it. The ratio is displayed and enters no forecast math.

Not modeled

The cash response. There is no applicable-percentage table, no benchmark plan premium, and no subsidy or Medicaid dollar amount anywhere in this engine — %FPL is a displayed ratio and enters no forecast math.
