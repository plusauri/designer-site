Alternative Minimum Tax

Exemption, phaseout threshold, and the 26% / 28% rate break by filing status.

Published 2022–2023Indexed

Source: Form 6251 — Alternative Minimum Tax, Individuals — Internal Revenue Service

https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-6251

Where we use thisUsed in your forecast

  • Computed every forecast year and assessed as an explicit line whenever tentative minimum tax exceeds regular tax.
  • The exemption phases out at 25 cents per dollar of AMTI above the threshold, which is what pulls higher-income years into it.

Not modeled

Full Form 6251 preferences and adjustments (ISO bargain element, private-activity bond interest, large SALT); AMT foreign tax credit; AMT NOL; prior-year minimum tax credit.
