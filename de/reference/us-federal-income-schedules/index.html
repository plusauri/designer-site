US federal income tax schedules

Ordinary, long-term capital gain, and qualified-dividend brackets plus the standard deduction, for every filing status.

Published 2022 · 2023 · 2026Indexed

Source: Rev. Proc. 2025-32 — inflation-adjusted items for tax year 2026 — Internal Revenue Service

https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf

Where we use thisUsed in your forecast

  • Assessed every forecast year to produce the household's annual federal tax statement. Ordinary income, long-term gains, and qualified dividends each walk their own ladder.
  • The standard deduction is subtracted before the ordinary ladder is walked.
  • Bracket edges are what a fill-to-bracket Roth conversion aims at, and what the Cliff radar draws its bracket lines from.
  • Between published years, thresholds are indexed forward at the plan's bracket-inflation rate.

Not modeled

Itemized deductions, credits, above-the-line adjustments beyond those the engine models, and any bracket year between publications (those are indexed forward from the nearest published table rather than looked up).
