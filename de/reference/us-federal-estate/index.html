Federal estate tax

The IRC §2001(c) unified rate schedule and the per-person basic exclusion amount.

Published 2025Indexed

Source: IRC §2001(c) — unified transfer-tax rate schedule — U.S. Code

https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/2001

Where we use this · Used in your forecast

  • Applied once at the end of the projection, as a transform over final net worth — not inside the Monte Carlo run.
  • Uses the unified-credit method: the tax on the whole estate minus the tax on the exclusion.
  • Only runs when the plan opts into estate tax; the estate card shows the full derivation.

Not modeled

Portability of a deceased spouse's unused exclusion; lifetime gift offsets; trust structures; generation-skipping transfer tax.
