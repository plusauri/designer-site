This lesson introduces a high-net-worth dual-earner sample for exploring concentration risk, fat right tails, and Banks-family follow-ons (forced RMDRequired Minimum Distribution — once you reach an age set by statute, the law makes you withdraw a minimum amount each year from traditional retirement accounts, whether or not you need the money. The withdrawal is taxable income, so RMDs can push a household into a higher bracket or over a surcharge threshold. Roth accounts are generally not subject to them.Full entry →, Reveal). Teaching claim: concentration + vol widens the Monte CarloThe method behind a multi-path run: draw many randomized return paths, run the plan down each one, and read the spread of what happened. Named after the casino, because the engine is repeated random sampling rather than a formula — which is why two runs of one plan differ a little, and why a success rate is a proportion of sampled futures rather than a promise.Full entry → cloud. Mean terminal NW often sits well above the medianThe middle outcome — half above, half below — as against the average, which a few enormous results can drag upward. Over long horizons the two come apart badly, and the median is the one that answers "what does a typical path look like?"Full entry → (fat right tail).
