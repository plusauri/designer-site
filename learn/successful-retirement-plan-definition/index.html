A success rate is only as meaningful as the rule that labels a path a success. Change the rule, and the same Monte CarloThe method behind a multi-path run: draw many randomized return paths, run the plan down each one, and read the spread of what happened. Named after the casino, because the engine is repeated random sampling rather than a formula — which is why two runs of one plan differ a little, and why a success rate is a proportion of sampled futures rather than a promise.Full entry → cloud can look safer or scarier — without changing a single dollar of spend or a single return draw.

Key Forecasts ↔ Distribution (how success looks)

Selecting a Key Forecasts row (Min, Max, GMF ✦GMF ✦ (also FID 0 / GMF) is the product north star — the deterministic geometric mean forecast path you steer by, not a random Monte Carlo draw and not “most likely.” Prefer it for a stable year-by-year read of the plan. Switch away when you want to explore other randomized scenarios or paths in the multi-path cloud. The ✦ mark matches the live FID control (compass / north star, not a rating star).Full entry →, quartiles, …) makes that path active everywhere — Forecast Detail follows it, and the matching mark on the Net Worth Distribution is highlighted.

  1. Select Min. The minimum is always the leftmost dot in the distribution (Max is always the rightmost). Hover Min — the tip typically labels the path a Failure (on this lean sample, Min is a hard-fail ending). Prefer Close up on the distribution if scrubbing is cramped.
  2. Scrub across many dots. Some succeed, some fail — the cloud is not one verdict.
  3. Dot color tracks quartile (poor → strong ending bands), not “success vs fail” by itself.
  4. The red rectangular band (when present) is negative ending net worth. Regardless of Gold / Silver / Bronze, finishing negative is a failure — every mark inside that band fails.
  5. Not every mark to the right of the red band is a success. Just outside the red you may hover paths whose tip says Liquidity Failure (or similar): short of cash in some year, borrowed to stay afloat, then bounced back. The plan charged for that borrowing via Cost of capital (next to the Definition of success under AssumptionsA main-rail area. The rules the forecast runs under — growth, taxes, movements, success definition, locality, currency. Read and write.). Under Silver, those recovered paths can still count as success; under Gold (Strict) they do not — intermediate liquidity stress is enough to fail even if the ending NW looks fine.

That is the whole point of the tier: Gold is the super-safe / conservative bar — no intermediate failure types allowed. Success % falls, but the number you keep should feel more trustworthy because the gate is stricter.

The definition of success can be changed in two places, Assumptions and Transforms. The previews below are full-width, because table cells shrink the chrome too much to read the three success choices.

The definition of success

Durable definition of success on the dossier. Pick Strict, Liquidity crises allowed, or Temporary negative net worth allowed. Cost of capital sits on the same panel — the annual rate charged when the plan must borrow to cover a shortfall.
