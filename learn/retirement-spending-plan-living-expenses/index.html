If I raise or lower lifestyle spend — in the Work years, the Retire years, or both — does the plan respond in a way I can see (cash-flow bars, success rate, late-life wealth)?

Living spend is one of the few levers households actually control day to day. Retirement age is hard to move; a few percent on the budget is a conversation you can have this month.
