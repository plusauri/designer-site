survivor risk

Finance

The risk that one partner dies early and the household's income falls further than its costs. When a married partner dies, several things change at once. The survivor keeps the larger of the two Social Security benefits, and the smaller one stops. A pension may stop or shrink, depending on the survivor option chosen when it began. Costs fall too, but usually by much less than half: the house, the car and the insurance cost about the same for one. Taxes change a year or two later. After the year of death, a survivor without a dependent child files as single, and the single brackets and standard deduction are about half the joint ones. The same income is then taxed at higher rates, which is often called the widow's penalty. Required distributions from a large traditional IRA can make it worse. Before retirement, the risk is the lost paycheck, which is what life insurance is for. In Tutilis, each person's death age is set under People and can be moved in Transforms, so a plan can be run with one partner dying early.

Où le trouver dans l'application

Où le saisir

  • Each person's death age; an earlier one for a partner runs the plan for a survivor.

Où l'essayer en scénario

  • Move one partner's death age earlier and compare the plan with and without it.
