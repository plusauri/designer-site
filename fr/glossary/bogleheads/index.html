Bogleheads

Community study

This page describes the Bogleheads investment philosophy as the Bogleheads community has written it down, on its forum and wiki at bogleheads.org. The community's own page is Bogleheads investment philosophy. Bogleheads® is a registered trademark of The John C. Bogle Center for Financial Literacy. Finance

Investors who follow the ideas of John C. Bogle (1929–2019), who founded Vanguard in 1975 and in 1976 launched the first index fund sold to individual investors. The community grew from an online forum that began on Morningstar in 1998 and later moved to its own home at bogleheads.org, which also hosts a wiki. The philosophy it writes down there is a short list: make a workable plan, invest early and often, never take too much or too little risk, diversify, never try to time the market, use index funds when possible, keep costs low, keep taxes low, keep it simple, and stay the course. The three-fund portfolio is the best-known way the community puts those ideas into practice. Tutilis describes the community's ideas and tests them on plans. It does not speak for the community, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the community or the John C. Bogle Center for Financial Literacy, which owns the Bogleheads® name.
