sales tax

Finance

A tax on what is bought, charged at the register as a share of the price. Each state sets its own rate, most localities add their own, and many states exempt groceries; five states levy no statewide sales tax. In Tutilis it is charged on living expenses at the state rate plus the average local rate, on the fifth of spending a sales tax typically reaches, plus groceries where the state taxes them. A plan can set its own share and rate under Assumptions, Taxes.

Où le trouver dans l'application

Où le saisir

  • The share of your spending sales tax reaches. Empty uses 20%, plus 8% for groceries where your state taxes them.

  • Your own state and local rate. Empty uses your state's rate plus its average local rate.

Où il apparaît dans la prévision

  • The year's sales tax on lifestyle spending: living expenses, the taxable part and the state plus average local rate.

  • Shade the states that levy a sales tax, and read each state's rate on its profile card.
