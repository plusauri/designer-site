Additional Medicare Tax

0.9% on earned income above a filing-status threshold. Statutory dollars, never indexed.

Nominal

Source: Form 8959 — Additional Medicare Tax — Internal Revenue Service

https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-8959

Where we use thisUsed in your forecast

  • The 0.9% rate is assessed on earned income above the threshold, as a separate line beside the base 1.45% Medicare tax.
  • Because the thresholds are never indexed, a growing wage drifts into them over a long forecast; the Cliff radar shows when.

Not modeled

Employer withholding mechanics; RRTA; self-employment nuances beyond applying the rate to the engine's earned-income bucket.
