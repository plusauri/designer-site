Historical crash episodes

The five market crashes a sequence-of-returns test can replay, with each year's stock and Treasury return in order.

Not year-keyed

Source: Robert J. Shiller, Irrational Exuberance data (ie_data.xls) — Robert J. Shiller, Yale University

https://shillerdata.com/

Where we use thisUsed in comparisons

  • Replayed only inside a what-if: SOR Risk in Transforms, or --overlay on the command line. The baseline forecast never reads them.
  • Each episode replays its measured annual returns exactly; every other category moves through its correlation with stocks and Treasuries, and years after the episode ends are sampled as usual.
