Medicare IRMAA tiers

CMS income-related Part B and Part D premium surcharges, keyed to MAGI from two years prior.

Published 2022 · 2023 · 2026Held

Source: Medicare Part B and Part D Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amounts — Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets

Where we use thisUsed in your forecast

  • Charged as a healthcare outflow in the forecast year — not as a tax — driven by MAGI from two years earlier.
  • Counted once per household member who has reached 65 in that year.
  • The Cliff radar shows how close a year's MAGI sits to the next tier, since crossing by one dollar costs the whole step.
  • The standard Part B premium on each table is shown for context and is deliberately not charged.

Not modeled

Life-changing-event appeals; state buy-ins; the harsher married-filing-separately "lived with spouse" table. The standard Part B premium is shown for context but is not charged as IRMAA.
