ACA marketplace interaction

What MAGI does to marketplace premium tax credits — and the honest limit of what this engine models about it.

Not year-keyed

Source: HHS Poverty Guidelines — U.S. Department of Health and Human Services / ASPE

https://aspe.hhs.gov/topics/poverty-economic-mobility/poverty-guidelines

Where we use this · Shown, not applied

  • A warning is surfaced for any year with positive MAGI while a marketplace-shaped healthcare phase is active.
  • No premium tax credit is computed, and no healthcare cost changes as a result.

Not modeled

The subsidy dollars themselves, and the ACA's own MAGI definition (which adds back tax-exempt interest, excluded foreign earned income, and untaxed Social Security in ways federal MAGI does not).
