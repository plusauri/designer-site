Social Security wage base & payroll rates

OASDI taxable maximum by year, the 6.2% / 1.45% employee rates, and the self-employment equivalents.

Published 2022 · 2023 · 2026Indexed

Source: Social Security Contribution and Benefit Base — Social Security Administration

https://www.ssa.gov/oact/cola/cbb.html

Where we use this · Used in your forecast

  • The 6.2% OASDI and 1.45% Medicare rates are assessed on earned income in every forecast year.
  • The wage base caps OASDI: earnings above it pay no Social Security tax. Transforms can raise, replace, or eliminate that cap as a what-if.
  • Self-employment income is assessed at the combined employer + employee rates on 92.35% of net earnings, and half of the result is deducted.
