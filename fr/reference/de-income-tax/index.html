German income tax (Einkommensteuer)

The progressive ESt ladder, the Abgeltungsteuer flat rate on capital income, and the solidarity surcharge.

Not year-keyed

Source: Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG) — Tarif and Abgeltungsteuer — Bundesministerium der Justiz / gesetze-im-internet.de

https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/

Where we use this · Used in your forecast

  • Assessed each forecast year for plans in the Germany planning region, in place of the US federal calculator.
  • Capital income is taxed at the flat Abgeltungsteuer rate rather than the progressive ladder; the solidarity surcharge rides the progressive tax only.

Not modeled

Filing status (Splittingtarif), church tax, Sparer-Pauschbetrag, and the Freigrenze mechanics of the solidarity surcharge.
