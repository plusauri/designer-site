State estate & inheritance tax

The dozen-odd states that levy a death tax, with their exemptions and top rates — separate from the federal estate tax.

Published 2025Held

Where we use thisUsed in your forecast

  • Assessed alongside the federal estate tax at the end of the projection, for the state of residence.
  • Where a precise graduated ladder is bundled it is used directly; where only a top rate is known, the estate card labels the result as an approximation.

Not modeled

Beneficiary-class detail beyond the modeled ladders, portability between spouses at the state level, trust structures, and the interaction between a state's estate tax and its inheritance tax where a state levies both.
