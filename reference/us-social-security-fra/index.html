Social Security full retirement age

FRA by birth cohort, the earliest claim age, and the early-reduction and delayed-credit rates that move a benefit off it.

Not year-keyed

Source: Normal Retirement Age by year of birth — Social Security Administration

https://www.ssa.gov/oact/ProgData/nra.html

Where we use thisUsed in your forecast

  • Sets when a modeled Social Security benefit starts and what fraction of the PIA it pays.
  • Claiming before full retirement age reduces the benefit permanently; claiming after adds delayed credits until they stop at 70.
  • Age 62 is the earliest a modeled benefit can begin; the plan's claiming choice is clamped into that window.
  • The claim-age timeline in the person editor is drawn from this table.

Not modeled

Spousal, survivor, and dependent benefits; the earnings test; WEP/GPO; the January-1 birthday rule is applied but no other day-level precision exists.
