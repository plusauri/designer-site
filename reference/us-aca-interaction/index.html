ACA marketplace interaction

What MAGI does to marketplace premium tax credits, and how this engine prices the credit for a plan bought on the marketplace.

Not year-keyed

Source: HHS Poverty Guidelines — U.S. Department of Health and Human Services / ASPE

https://aspe.hhs.gov/topics/poverty-economic-mobility/poverty-guidelines

Where we use thisShown, not applied

  • For a healthcare phase marked as a marketplace plan, the credit is the benchmark premium less the applicable percentage of ACA household income (AGI with all Social Security and tax-exempt interest added back), never more than the premium. From 2026 no credit is paid above 400% of the poverty line.
  • The credit is paid in advance on the income the year's flows project and reconciled on the year's return; excess advance is repaid in full with the next year's tax.
  • An unmarked phase that looks like marketplace coverage gets a warning for any year with positive MAGI, and no credit.

Not modeled

Medicaid (below 138% of the poverty line in expansion states), an employer offer that rules the credit out, cost-sharing reductions, state subsidies, excluded foreign earned income in ACA household income, and any phase not marked as a marketplace plan, which gets a warning instead of a credit.
