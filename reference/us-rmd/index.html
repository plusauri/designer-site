Required minimum distributions

SECURE 2.0 start age by birth year and the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table divisors that set the amount.

Not year-keyed

Source: Publication 590-B — Distributions from Individual Retirement Arrangements — Internal Revenue Service

https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b

Where we use this · Used in your forecast

  • From the start age onward, a required distribution is forced out of every eligible pre-tax account each forecast year, whether or not the plan needed the cash.
  • The amount is the prior year-end balance divided by the Uniform Lifetime divisor for the age reached that year.
  • Forced distributions become ordinary income on the tax statement and show up as forced sales in the sales plan.
  • The 25% excise applies to any shortfall against the required amount.

Not modeled

Inherited-account and beneficiary tables; the joint-life table for a spouse more than 10 years younger; still-working exceptions; reduced excise rates for timely correction.
