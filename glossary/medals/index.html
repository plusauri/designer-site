medals (Gold / Silver / Bronze)

Tutilis

The product's three success gates, strictest first: Gold, Silver, Bronze. They let you read a plan's health without arguing about one success percentage — a plan that clears Bronze but not Silver is telling you exactly which margin is thin. The gates test the success definition you chose, so changing that changes the medal.
