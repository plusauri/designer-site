growth

Financial

How fast a value changes from year to year. For an investment it is the same idea as return. Tutilis also gives growth to things that are not investments: a home or other real asset appreciates, and a cash flow such as a salary or an expense rises with inflation or its own rate. Each carries its own growth assumption.

Where it is in the app

Where you enter it

  • A home or other real asset carries its own growth rate.

  • A cash flow that changes over time, such as a salary or an expense, carries its own growth rate.

  • The growth each kind of real asset starts from, before a plan sets its own.

  • The growth each kind of cash flow starts from, before a plan sets its own.

Where you try it as a what-if

  • Pick a real asset and vary its growth.

  • Pick a cash flow and vary its growth.
