correlation

Financial

How closely two investments tend to move together, from −1 (opposite) through 0 (unrelated) to +1 (lockstep). Written ρ (rho). It is what decides whether holding several things actually spreads risk: assets that all fall at once are less diversified than a count of holdings suggests.
