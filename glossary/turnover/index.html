turnover

Financial

How much of a portfolio is sold and replaced in a year. Some planning tools ask for an "account turnover rate" and use it to estimate how much gain is realized, and taxed, each year in a taxable account. Tutilis does not model turnover. The nearest input is a holding's capital gain distribution yield, and the two are not the same thing. Turnover realizes a share of the unrealized gain: what it costs depends on how far the holding's value sits above its cost basis, and each sale raises the basis, so a high-turnover account carries little untaxed gain. A distribution yield is a share of market value, paid whatever the basis is. So the same percentage realizes less gain as turnover than as a distribution yield, and far less when the basis is close to the value. The two come closest for a fund held a long time, with a low basis.

Where it is in the app

Tutilis does not model portfolio turnover. The nearest input is a holding's capital gain distribution yield, which is a share of market value rather than of unrealized gain.
