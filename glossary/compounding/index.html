compounding

Financial

Growth on top of previous growth — this year's return is earned on last year's gains as well as the original money. It is why a plan curve bends upward instead of climbing in a straight line, and why the early years matter more than their size suggests.

Where it is in the app

Where it shows in the forecast

  • Grows a yearly amount over a range of years, so you can watch compounding work.

  • Hover a holding: its median path, with a risk cone out to the plan horizon.
