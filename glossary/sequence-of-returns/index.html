sequence of returns

Financial

The order the good and bad years arrive in. Two plans with identical average returns can end in very different places if one takes its losses early while money is being withdrawn — selling into a fall removes shares that are then not there for the recovery. It is why retirement risk is not summarized by an average, and why the years either side of the day you stop working carry more weight than their count suggests.

Where it is in the app

Where you try it as a what-if

  • Lays a historical crash over the plan's early years, starting in a year you choose.

Where it shows in the forecast

  • Each item's return, year by year, on the forecast path: a crash what-if shows as the dip, and the recovery after it.
