hyperinflation

Financial

A rise in prices so fast that money stops working as a way to hold value. Philip Cagan's 1956 line is a month when prices rise 50 percent or more. Germany in 1923, Hungary in 1946, Zimbabwe in 2008, and Venezuela in the 2010s passed it. A grocery slider on a household plan is a stress for one bill, and it stops far below a month like that.

Where it is in the app

Inflation is modeled at ordinary rates. The Guardrails study's inflation slider stops near the 1980 peak.
