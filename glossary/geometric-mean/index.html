geometric mean

Financial

The compound growth rate that actually takes a starting balance to an ending one — the return you lived through rather than the one you were quoted. It is always at or below the arithmetic mean, equal only if every year is identical, and further below as volatility rises. This is the mean the product's north-star path is built on.
