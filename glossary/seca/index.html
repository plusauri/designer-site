SECA

Financial

Self-Employment Contributions Act — the self-employed counterpart to FICA. With no employer to pay half, you owe both halves on net self-employment earnings, so the same dollar of income carries a higher payroll-tax rate than wage income does. This is why plans model wage income and self-employment income as different kinds of earnings rather than one.
