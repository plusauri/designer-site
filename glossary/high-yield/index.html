high yield

Financial

A bond — usually corporate — rated below investment grade, paying a higher interest rate because the borrower is more likely to default. The name sounds like good news; the trap is that high-yield bonds tend to fall alongside stocks in a sell-off, so the “bond” label does not make them a diversifier. Also called junk bonds or credit.
