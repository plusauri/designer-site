catch-up contribution

Financial

Extra room to contribute to a retirement account once you reach a set age — the ordinary limit plus an additional amount, with a still-higher amount in a narrow age band. The name is aspirational: nothing has to be caught up on to use it.

Where it is in the app

Where it shows in the forecast

  • Shown in each year a person is 50 or older: the extra amount they may contribute.

  • Ages 60 through 63: the larger catch-up.
