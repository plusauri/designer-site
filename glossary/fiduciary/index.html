Fiduciary

Financial

Someone legally bound to act in your best interest across the whole relationship, putting your interests ahead of their own. A registered investment adviser owes this duty for all the advice it gives. A broker owes a narrower best-interest duty under Regulation Best Interest, which applies when it recommends a security or a strategy rather than for the life of the account. Form CRS says in plain words which standard a firm is held to.
