expense ratio

Financial

What a fund charges each year to run it, as a percent of what you hold: management, administration, and the other costs of the fund. A 0.04% expense ratio costs $4 a year on every $10,000. It is taken out of the fund's assets day by day, so it never arrives as a bill and never appears on a tax return. It shows up only as a return slightly lower than the fund's holdings earned. Because the fee comes out every year on a growing balance, its cost compounds over decades the way the returns do. Broad index funds commonly charge well under 0.1% a year. Many actively managed funds charge 0.5% to 1%, for the work of choosing what to hold. A fund's prospectus and its fact sheet both state the figure. In Tutilis, the expense ratio is set on the holding card and shared by every holding of that fund. The forecast takes it out of the holding's value after each year's growth, and the year's total shows as Fund expenses (not a cash flow) under the Cash Flow statement.

Where it is in the app

Where you enter it

  • Set once per fund; every holding of that fund shares it.

Where it shows in the forecast

  • Below Net: the fee lowered the holdings' value and moved no cash.
