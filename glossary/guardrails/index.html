guardrails

Financial

Spending rules that react to how the plan is doing — raise spending when the portfolio runs ahead, trim it when it falls behind — instead of holding one number for thirty years. They trade a perfectly steady income for a much smaller chance of running out, and they are the practical answer to sequence of returns risk. Tutilis never applies guardrails in your forecast, which spends what your cash flows and residual strategy say. The Guardrails panel runs the published rules on a simple side path beside your plan, so you can compare them.

Where it is in the app

Where it shows in the forecast

  • A study beside the plan; it does not change it.

  • The smallest starting portfolio that keeps the study's coverage buffer funded.

  • How far today's investable nest egg sits below Min portfolio.

  • Red years are short of funding under the selected study's spending.
