tax-free

Financial

No income tax due when the money comes out — the Roth promise, and the reason Roth dollars keep a withdrawal clear of income-driven thresholds. The word hides a condition: the withdrawal has to be qualified, which turns on age and how long the account has been open. It also hides a scope: municipal-bond interest is tax-free on the federal return yet still counts toward whether Social Security is taxed.
