wealth tax

Financial

A tax on what a household owns rather than on what it earns: a percentage of net worth above an exemption, charged whether or not the assets produced any income that year. The United States has no federal wealth tax, though proposals recur. A few countries levy one, usually at a low rate on large fortunes. In Tutilis it exists only as a what-if in Transforms. You choose the rate, how often it is charged (once, every few years, or every year) and the exemption, and you can leave the home and retirement accounts out. It is paid the year after it is assessed, like other taxes, and shows as its own cash-flow line.
