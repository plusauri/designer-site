σ (sigma)

Financial

The Greek letter the product uses for volatility — how widely returns scatter around their average. A bare σ on a chart axis is measuring risk, not return: two holdings can sit at the same height (same μ) and be completely different things to live through. Higher σ also drags the growth you actually compound below the average you were quoted.
