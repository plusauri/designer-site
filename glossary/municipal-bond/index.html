municipal bond

Financial

Debt issued by a state, city, or local authority. The federal exclusion is real but narrow, and the word *tax-exempt* oversells it in three directions. It is excluded from federal AGI, so it does not appear as ordinary income on Form 1040. It is not excluded from MAGI, the figure that drives IRMAA Medicare premiums, the NIIT, and how much of a Social Security check is taxable — so muni interest can raise costs that never show up as income tax. And on a state return, most states that levy an income tax add back interest from out-of-state issuers, so a national fund is largely taxable where you live. The exclusion is also account-sensitive: inside a traditional IRA or 401(k) it never happens at all, because withdrawals are ordinary income regardless of what the account held.
