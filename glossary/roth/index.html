Roth

Financial

A retirement account where you pay income tax on the money going in, and qualified withdrawals later come out tax-free — the mirror image of traditional. Named after the senator behind the law, so the word tells you nothing on its own. Plans care because Roth dollars do not add to taxable income later, which keeps them clear of income-driven cliffs.
