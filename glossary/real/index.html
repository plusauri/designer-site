real

Financial

Adjusted for inflation — a purchasing-power figure rather than a face-value one. The counterpart to nominal, and the more honest of the two over a long plan: a balance that triples in nominal dollars across thirty years may buy only a little more than it does now. Always check which one a chart is drawn in before comparing a future number to today's prices.
