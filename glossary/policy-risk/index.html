policy risk

Financial

The risk that a law changes the rules a plan was built on. A retirement plan runs for decades, and the rules under it change several times along the way. Tax brackets and rates are set by Congress and some are written to expire. Social Security benefits depend on a trust fund that is projected to run short in the 2030s. The age for required minimum distributions has been raised twice since 2019, and a third rise is already written into law. A new tax, such as a wealth tax, is proposed from time to time. No one can forecast the next law, but a plan can be tested against the changes most often discussed. The plans most exposed are those that lean heavily on one benefit, those with large traditional balances whose tax is still to be paid, and wealthy households for whom a new tax is aimed. In Tutilis, Transforms carries the changes you can test: a Social Security shortfall, a wealth tax, and a change to the Social Security wage base.
