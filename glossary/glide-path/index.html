glide path

Financial

A planned shift in allocation over time — classically toward bonds as retirement approaches, so risk comes down as the years available to recover from a loss come down with it. The word that matters is *planned*: a portfolio drifting toward whatever has been winning is not on a glide path, it is unrebalanced.
