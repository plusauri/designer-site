present value

Financial

What a future amount is worth today, after discounting for the years between. A multi-year outflow shown as one present value is not a sum of its rows — a dollar due in twenty years counts for less than one due next year. The product's PV rollups and "amount mode" switches are this idea applied to a whole flow.
