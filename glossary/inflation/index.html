inflation

Financial

A rise in the price level. The same basket of goods costs more dollars than it did. A household plan carries a growth rate on each bill, because one published inflation number is one basket. The reporting rate that restates future dollars into today's dollars is a separate assumption.

Where it is in the app

Where you enter it

  • This plan has no single inflation rate: each cash flow carries its own growth, and for an expense that growth is its inflation. A salary's growth also includes raises.

  • The plan-wide rates: Report inflation restates future dollars as today's dollars, and Bracket indexing sets how fast tax brackets follow prices. Set them in the assumption set, on the library page.

  • What future dollars are restated in when amounts show in Today's $. It applies to every plan opened with this assumption set.

  • How fast tax brackets and the standard deduction follow prices. Below inflation, the brackets creep.

  • The growth each kind of cash flow starts from, such as health care or education, before a plan sets its own.

Where you try it as a what-if

  • Pick an expense and raise its growth to ask what happens if its prices run hot.

Where it shows in the forecast

  • Inflation for the Guardrails study; it does not change the plan.
