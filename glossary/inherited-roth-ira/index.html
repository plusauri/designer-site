Inherited Roth IRA

Financial

A Roth IRA held by someone who inherited it. Qualified withdrawals stay tax-free, but unlike the original owner's Roth it must be emptied on the beneficiary schedule. Because a Roth owner is always treated as dying before the required beginning date, most heirs owe nothing until the tenth year, which lets the money grow untouched until the deadline.
