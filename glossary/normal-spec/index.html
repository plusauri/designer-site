Normal Spec

Tutilis

How a growth assumption is entered: a normal distribution, written N(μ, σ) — an expected return and a volatility. "Normal" is the bell curve, not "ordinary": it is the shape the draws come from. The editor also shows μg, the geometric mean (μ − σ²/2), because that compounded rate — not μ — is what the projection actually uses.

Where it is in the app

Where you enter it

  • One holding's own growth.

  • The defaults every holding of a category starts from.
