Roth conversion

Financial

Deliberately moving money from a traditional account into a Roth one and paying the income tax on it now, so it grows and comes out tax-free later. The usual reason is a low-income year — between retiring and claiming benefits, say — where the conversion is taxed at a lower rate than the future withdrawal would be. It also shrinks later RMDs.

Where it is in the app

Where you enter it

  • Turn the policy on, then choose a fixed yearly amount or fill to a bracket.

Where it shows in the forecast

  • Each year's conversion is a row whose Why reads Roth conversion.
