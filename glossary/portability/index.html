portability

Financial

A surviving spouse's right to add to their own federal estate-tax exemption whatever exemption the first spouse to die did not use. The amount carried over is called the deceased spousal unused exclusion. It is not automatic. The executor must elect it on a federal estate-tax return (Form 706) filed on time after the first death, even when the estate is small enough that no tax is due. A missed election cannot usually be recovered later. Portability is a federal rule. Most states with their own estate tax do not allow it, so a couple can lose one spouse's state exemption at the first death unless the estate plan uses a trust to hold it.
