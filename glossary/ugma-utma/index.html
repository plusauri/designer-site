UGMA / UTMA

Financial

Custodial accounts — Uniform Gifts / Transfers to Minors Act — that hold money in a child's name with an adult managing it until the child comes of age. Simple to open, but the money is the child's outright, and for financial aid it counts as a student asset, which is assessed far more heavily than a parent's.

Where it is in the app

There is no custodial UGMA/UTMA account type. The nearest is a taxable account.
