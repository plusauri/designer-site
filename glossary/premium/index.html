premium

Financial

What you pay for coverage — a monthly insurance or Medicare cost — and not a bonus or a prize. Confusingly, the same word means "above face value" when applied to a bond. In a plan it is nearly always the insurance sense: a recurring outflow that rises with age and, past a threshold, with income.
