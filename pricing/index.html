Try it free for 30 days

When the store opens, you can download Tutilis free and use all of it for 30 days. There is no card to enter: you check out at $0 with Lemon Squeezy, which emails you a link to the installers, the same way a purchase does.

The 30 days start the first time you open the app. Until then, nothing is counting down.

After 30 days, your plans stay on your computer and still open. You can read them and try different assumptions, but changing the plan itself needs a license key. Buy one during the trial or after it, paste the key into the app, and you carry on where you left off, with nothing to reinstall.

Purchases are not open yet. This early site is for preview and feedback only. Checkout and license delivery will be wired through Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record) when we open the store. A second tier, Cogilis, which lets your own AI assistant work with the planning engine, is planned after launch.

Try the ideas first

While the store is closed, the Learning Center is the best way to learn the planning ideas and see Tutilis live: sample households, Monte Carlo, and lessons — teaching first, product second. Your own editable dossier remains a desktop-app concern when purchases open.

Questions

Which platforms? macOS and Windows desktop. Your plan and data live on your own machine.

Is it really one-time? Yes. Buy once and keep it, with no subscription. A year of updates is included; renewing for another year is optional, and the version you have never stops working.

How will licenses work? After checkout opens, Lemon Squeezy will email a license key. Paste it into the app to unlock it. The free trial needs no key.

What happens when the trial ends? Nothing is deleted. Your plans open read-only until you enter a license key.

Refunds? Handled through Lemon Squeezy when the store is live; a refund deactivates the license key.
