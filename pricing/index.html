Pricing

Buy it once and keep it. No subscription. Prices are in US dollars, before tax.

Purchases are not open yet. The store opens in November. This early site is for preview and feedback. Checkout and license delivery run through Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. Cogilis opens at the same time, as early access.

Tutilis (desktop)

The full desktop app for Mac and Windows: forecasting, tax-aware projections, side-by-side scenarios, and region analysis.

  • $99, a one-time purchase
  • Runs locally — your data never leaves your machine
  • 12 months of updates included: new features and next year's tax tables
  • After that, another year of updates is $49, and optional. The version you have keeps working either way
  • Free for the first 14 days

Cogilis — early access, bring your own AI

Cogilis lets an AI assistant you already use, such as Claude Code or Codex, answer questions about your own plans with the Tutilis engine. You ask in plain language. The assistant runs Tutilis's commands on your plans, reads the forecasts, and shows the commands behind every number.

Bring your own AI. Cogilis does not include an AI. You use your own assistant and pay its provider for it, as you would anyway. Tutilis itself sends nothing anywhere. Your assistant may send what it reads to its provider, under your agreement with them, so choose one you trust with your finances.

It is early access, for people comfortable at a terminal. If you have used an AI coding assistant or a command line, it is for you. If those words are new, start with Tutilis and the Learning Center. Cogilis will get easier to use, and early buyers get those improvements with their updates.

  • Everything in Tutilis, plus the AI workspace, which the app sets up for you
  • $149, a one-time purchase
  • 12 months of updates included, as with Tutilis. After that, another year is $69, and optional
  • The free trial includes it, so you can try it for 14 days before deciding
  • Not for you after all? A full refund within 30 days
  • It is a planning tool, not financial advice, and so is the assistant using it

Try it free for 14 days

You can download Tutilis free and use all of it for 14 days. There is no card to enter: you check out at $0 with Lemon Squeezy, which emails you a link to the installers, the same way a purchase does.

The 14 days start the first time you open the app. Until then, nothing is counting down.

After 14 days, your plans stay on your computer and still open. You can read them and try different assumptions, but changing the plan itself needs a license key. Buy one during the trial or after it, paste the key into the app, and you carry on where you left off, with nothing to reinstall.

Questions

Which platforms? macOS and Windows desktop. Your plan and data live on your own machine.

Is it really one-time? Yes. Buy once and keep it, with no subscription. A year of updates is included; renewing for another year is optional ($49 for Tutilis, $69 for Cogilis), and the version you have never stops working.

How do licenses work? After checkout, Lemon Squeezy emails a license key. Paste it into the app to unlock it. The free trial needs no key.

What happens when the trial ends? Nothing is deleted. Your plans open read-only until you enter a license key.

Refunds? Handled through Lemon Squeezy; a refund deactivates the license key.
