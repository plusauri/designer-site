Privacy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Plusauri, LLC runs this website, the Learning Center, and the Tutilis desktop app. Tutilis keeps your financial plan on your own computer. We never see it unless you choose to send us something. This page covers what our website records, what the app sends us when you write to us, and what we receive when you buy.

The short version

  • There are no third-party trackers, analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking pixels on this site or in our emails.
  • Our database does not store your IP address.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share what we record for advertising.
  • What we do record is listed below, field by field, and it is all kept by us.

When you read the Learning Center

Each time a lesson page opens, we record one row with:

  • which lesson, the page address, and the language it was shown in;
  • the time;
  • the description your browser sends with every request (its "user agent", such as "Safari on a Mac"), the languages your browser says you prefer, and the page that linked you here, if your browser shares it;
  • the invite code and a random browser number, if you arrived through one of our invite links (see the next section);
  • whether the visit was one of our own test visits.

We use this to learn which lessons people read and where they stop, so we can make them better.

Invite links

When we write to someone about a lesson, we often send a personal link of the form /o/some-code. Each code is made for one person or one post, so we know who we sent it to.

When you open an invite link, we record the code, the lesson it points to, the time, your browser's description, its preferred languages, the page that linked you here, and a random browser number. Then we send you on to the lesson.

The same visit sets an attribution cookie, plusauri_o_attr. It holds the invite code and the random browser number, and nothing else. For about 30 days, the lesson pages you open on this site are credited to that invite. This is how we can tell that someone read several lessons over two sittings, instead of only knowing that they tapped the link. The cookie grants no access to anything. Clearing this site's cookies ends it.

What other companies' servers see

We do not send anyone information about you. Some pieces of a page are still fetched by your browser from other companies' servers, and those servers see your request the way any web server does, including your IP address:

  • jsDelivr, a public content network, serves the stylesheet that lays out these pages, and on some lessons the libraries that draw formulas and diagrams.
  • YouTube serves the player on the few lessons that include a video. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and the player loads only when you scroll to it. Once you play a video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
  • open.er-api.com answers if you press the button that fetches current exchange rates inside a lesson's sample plan. Nothing is fetched until you press it.
  • Render hosts our website and database in Oregon, in the United States. Like any host, it keeps standard request logs, which include IP addresses, under its own terms.

When you write to us

The Reach Out button in Tutilis and in the Learning Center sends us a message. We keep:

  • your message and the topic you chose;
  • any tables you chose to attach, and the names of any charts you attached (we do not keep the chart images);
  • the app version, whether you wrote from the desktop app or the Learning Center, your language, planning region, and license status;
  • your browser's description;
  • whether you offered an anonymized copy of a plan. The plan itself is not stored with your message.

If you email us directly, your email stays in our mailbox.

Surveys

When you answer one of our surveys, we keep your answers, the survey link's code, the name you give, your email address if you give one so we can follow up, your browser's description, and the time.

When you buy Tutilis

Lemon Squeezy runs our checkout and is the merchant of record, so you buy from them and their privacy policy covers the purchase. Your card details never reach us. After an order, Lemon Squeezy sends us a notice. From it we keep your name, your email address, the order and product, the price and currency, the order's status, and whether it was refunded. We also keep each notice as it arrived. We use this to stand behind your license and your updates, and for our own tax and accounting records.

Mailing list

We do not run a mailing list yet. When we start one, this page will say what it keeps and how to leave it.

How long we keep things

We do not delete these records on a schedule yet. We keep reading and messaging records while they help us improve the lessons and the product. We keep sales records as long as tax and accounting rules require.

If you would like a copy of what we hold about you, or would like it deleted, write to us. We will delete anything tied to you except the sales records we are required to keep.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
