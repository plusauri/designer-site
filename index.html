What the case studies cover

Financial planning first — the live plan is there when it helps, not as the only reason a topic exists. Most pages are a household with a question, worked through; a few teach a concept you need before the question makes sense.

Financial planning concepts

  • Basics — compound growth, diversification and correlation, risk and return, what Monte Carlo is (and is not) saying.
  • Reference / definitions — account-type differences, tax schedules and how they show up in a plan, IRMAA surcharges, medical / subsidy ideas, glossary entries.
  • Technical / applied — living spend, mortgage vs paid off, college funding, withdrawal order, guardrails, Roth conversion windows, wage-base caps, MAGI cliffs and two-year lookbacks, and other interactions you feel in a real plan.
