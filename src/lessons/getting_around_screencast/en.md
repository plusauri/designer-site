This is the **video** twin of [Reading Lesson (Getting Around)](/learn/getting_around).
Watch here; practice the same moves on the [[term:live-plan]] next to this lesson (Springfield —
the same teaching household as the reading tour).

The tour runs in **three parts**. They are meant to be watched in order, but each one stands on its
own, so you can stop after any of them and come back.

> **Everything in these videos, you can do right now.** The [[term:live-plan]] beside this lesson is
> the same product, on the same household, as the one on screen — every panel, chip, slider, and
> dialog you are about to watch me open is there and working. Nothing here is a mock-up or a
> recording standing in for the real thing.
>
> So please do not just watch. Pause whenever something looks interesting, do it yourself on the
> right, and then pick the video back up. Nothing you try can damage anything: the teaching dossier
> is read-only, and what-ifs in Transforms are a temporary comparison rather than a saved change.

The two panes, what a [[term:dossier]] is, and the six areas on the rail.
