<h4 id="saying-free-lunch">Saying: There's no free lunch. (TNFL)</h4>

- Id: `free-lunch`, written `<aside class="lesson-saying" data-saying="free-lunch">`, or `[[saying:free-lunch]]` as a chip
- Meaning: Anything that looks free is paid for somewhere: in risk, in fees, in taxes, or in a benefit you give up.
- The exception: Diversification comes closest. Spreading money across holdings that do not move together lowers the risk without lowering the expected return.

<h4 id="saying-small-leak">Saying: Small leaks sink great ships. (SLSGS)</h4>

- Id: `small-leak`, written `<aside class="lesson-saying" data-saying="small-leak">`, or `[[saying:small-leak]]` as a chip
- Meaning: A small cost that comes back every year, such as a fee, a tax or a habit, compounds into a large one.

<h4 id="saying-stay-the-course">Saying: Stay the course. (STC)</h4>
